What it is

yourCB turns the outfit inspiration you save from Pinterest and screenshots into a shoppable feed of real, in-stock pieces. I co-founded it with Aadreeka Srivastava, who leads brand, product and outreach. I built every layer of the product and shipped its closed-beta backend as sole engineer.

What I built

  1. The stack

    An Expo/React Native client in TypeScript; Supabase Postgres with pgvector and an HNSW index; a FastAPI service on Modal GPUs running 768-dimensional SigLIP image embeddings and Claude vision for moderation.

  2. The taste-learning loop

    One idempotent Postgres function over an append-only ledger, so repeated taps and retries never double-count. In a seeded test, three likes of one style raised its similarity from 0.006 to 0.28 and its share of the next feed from 11 to 19 items.

  3. A nightly catalog sync

    A retryable Trigger.dev job pulls about 8,400 products from 35 brands' public Shopify catalogs, allowlisted, robots.txt honoured, one request per second per store, and embeds each one.

  4. The closed-beta backend

    Shipped October 2026: row-level security on every user table, verified with a two-account test; GitHub Actions CI with Jest and pytest; an end-to-end test that includes full account deletion.

  5. Operations

    Sentry and PostHog. Recommendations are a pgvector query plus re-ranking in code; there is no separate vector database.
